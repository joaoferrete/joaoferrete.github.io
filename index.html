Projects

Things I've built.

Go Lister

Live

Published by GoWorks

A real-time collaborative music platform: one room, one link, and a queue everyone controls playing in sync. Kotlin/Spring Boot backend, three React frontends, and a Chromecast receiver.

KotlinSpring BootWebSocketRedisReact

The problem

Keeping several devices on the same second of the same song, when each provider exposes playback differently — and the browser won't let you authenticate a WebSocket properly.

The decision

The server owns the queue and the clock. The WebSocket is authenticated by ticket instead.

The consequence

nginx became the only ingress to the backend, with TLS terminated at Cloudflare.

Terminal Assistant

Notes, tasks and smart home under a single trigger engine, in one command. Automations are plain Python: your machine's own state drives your lights and your reminders.

PythonCLIHome AssistantSQLiteAutomation

The problem

Home Assistant does not know you just joined a call, and GNOME does not know how to turn on a lamp.

The decision

Its own trigger engine in Python, and one hard rule: no integration may be mandatory.

The consequence

The core runs anywhere Python runs and a file can be written.

lifter → Go Lifter

Google Play PyPI

Published by GoWorks

Two projects, one lineage: lifter, a training coach shipped as a Python CLI on PyPI, and Go Lifter, the native Android app in Kotlin and Compose that succeeds it — now available on Google Play.

PythonKotlinComposePyPIGoogle Fit

The problem

A coach is only useful if it remembers your history — and the minute it remembers, it can also ruin your data.

The decision

The coach proposes, it never writes: every change goes through your approval.

The consequence

The CLI shipped to PyPI with six interchangeable model providers and independent profiles.

Copyveil

Open source · v0.1

Published by GoWorks

A clipboard history for Linux that remembers what you copy and forgets your secrets. Tokens and passwords are masked, kept in memory only and erased after a short time; everything else is encrypted on disk.

RustGTK 4libadwaitaD-BusWayland

The problem

A clipboard manager keeps everything you copy, in plain text, for as long as it can.

The decision

Every item gets a sensitivity, and secrets never touch the disk.

The consequence

The guarantees live in code and CI, not in the README.
